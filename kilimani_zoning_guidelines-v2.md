## 1. Statutory Planning & Development Control Framework

### 1.1 Local Physical & Land Use Development Plan (LPLUDP) Types
Local Physical and Land Use Development Plans (LPLUDPs) are short-term spatial instruments (5 to 10 years planning horizon) under Section 45 of the Physical and Land Use Planning Act (PLUPA), 2019 [125, 126]. The statutory plan categories relevant to high-density urban nodes like Kilimani include:

1. **Action / Renewal / Redevelopment Plan:** Comprehensive planning for urban core areas undergoing intensive land use transformation, structural upgrading, high-rise redevelopment, or re-use of derelict urban land [126].
2. **Advisory / Zoning Plan:** Guidance map prescribing permitted land uses, building heights, plot ratios, plot coverage, subdivision limits, and density thresholds [126].
3. **Subject Plan:** In-depth treatment of a specific sector across the local area (e.g., Non-Motorized Transport networks, urban greening, or drainage reticulation) [126].
4. **Part Development Plan (PDP):** Site-specific allocation for immediate implementation of public projects, public land reservation, or regularization [127].
5. **Site Layout Plan:** Property-scale layout (Scale 1:2,500) showing exact boundaries, building footprints, setbacks, vehicular access, turning bays, and parking layouts [127, 128].
6. **Integrated Urban Development Plan:** Specific to cities and municipalities under Sections 38 to 41 of the Urban Areas and Cities Act (UACA Amendment 2019), subject to PLUPA approval [129].

### 1.3 Development Control Workflow & Application Requirements

#### 1.3.1 Key Development Applications [161–170]
1. **Change of User / Extension of User / Extension of Lease [163]:**
   * **Required Documentation:** Completed application form, certified Title Deed / Lease Certificate, official land search certificate, land rate clearance certificate, geo-referenced cadastral map, location plan, national newspaper notice, on-site public notice board, and a Planning Brief prepared by a registered physical planner [163].
   * **Approving Authority:** County Executive Committee Member (CECM) in charge of Physical Planning [164].
   * **Technical Circulation:** 14-day circulation to NEMA, Director of Survey, Water & Sewerage authorities, Public Health, and Urban Management Authorities [163, 164].
2. **Subdivision and Amalgamation [164, 165]:**
   * **Required Documentation:** Application form, subdivision scheme plan signed by a registered physical planner, geo-referenced cadastral map, official search certificate, ownership documents, and Planning Brief [164, 165].
   * **Approving Authority:** CECM / Cabinet Secretary [165].
3. **Building Plans Approval [166, 167]:**
   * **Required Documentation:** Architectural & structural drawings by registered professionals, electrical & mechanical/plumbing engineering drawings, geotechnical survey report, ownership documents, NEMA license, and NCA compliance certificate [166].

#### 1.3.2 Planning Brief Structure [183–185]
Every application for change of user, extension of user, extension of lease, subdivision, or site layout plan must include a Planning Brief containing [184, 185]:
1. Precise Title & Land Reference (LR) Number [184].
2. Context of Application (policy, legal, regulatory, zoning provisions) [184].
3. Project Description (nature, scope, character, additional service demands) [185].
4. Justification & Potential Opportunities [185].
5. Impact Assessment & Mitigation Strategies (traffic, sewer, water, waste, urban form) [185].
6. Statutory Attachments (ownership docs, searches, rate clearances, notices, site plans) [185].

## 2. Residential & Mixed-Use Density Regulations

Kilimani is characterized as a high-density, mixed-use commercial and residential node undergoing rapid urban transition. Planning parameters strictly control plot sizes, plot coverage, plot ratios, building lines, setbacks, and parking ratios.

### 2.1 Residential Density Typologies & Development Ratios [458–462]

| Density Zone / Typology | Permitted Building Typologies | Minimum Plot Size | Maximum Plot Coverage | Permitted Plot Ratio | Dwelling Unit Thresholds |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Low Density** | Single-family Bungalow / Maisonette | 0.20 hectares (2,000 square metres) | 30% | 1:2 (50%) | 1 to 4 units per plot |
| **Medium Density** | Detached / Semi-Detached Bungalow / Maisonette / Townhouses | 0.10 hectares (1,000 square metres) | 50% | 1:3 to 1:4 (300% to 400%) | 5 to 20 units per plot |
| **High Density (Kilimani Standard)** | Multi-storey Flats & High-Rise Apartments | 0.045 hectares (450 square metres) | 70% | 1:5 to 1:8 (500% to 800%) | 20+ units per plot |
| **Mixed Density / Comprehensive Scheme** | High-Rise Apartments, Townhouses, Integrated Commercial | 0.045 hectares (450 square metres) | 70% | 1:5 to 1:8 (500% to 800%) | Comprehensive Multi-unit |

### 2.2 Minimum Setback & Building Line Standards [459, 462, 463]

Building lines set the limit beyond which structures cannot extend toward streets; setbacks specify minimum open clearances from property boundaries [462].

| Residential Zone Density | Front Building Line / Setback | Side Boundary Setback | Rear Boundary Setback | Permitted Uses within Setbacks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Low Density** | 6.0 metres | 3.0 metres | 6.0 metres | **Front:** Parking & greening [463]<br>**Side:** Soft landscape greening [463]<br>**Rear:** Parking & soft landscaping [463] |
| **Medium Density** | 6.0 metres | 2.0 metres | 4.0 metres | Same as above [463] |
| **High Density (Kilimani Node)** | 6.0 metres | 2.0 metres | 2.0 metres | Same as above [463] |

#### Additional Residential Development Rules [457, 460–466]:
* **Perimeter Wall Height:** Perimeter boundary walls must not exceed **2.1 metres in height** to promote natural ventilation, light, and urban aesthetics [460].
* **Setback Unobstructions:** All setback spaces must remain completely open and unobstructed from ground level upwards [460].
* **Building Orientation:** Orient structures in relation to solar path and prevailing winds, respecting natural land contours [465].
* **Servants' Quarters & Outbuildings:** Must be sited to preserve privacy for main dwelling occupants [465, 466].
* **Soft Landscaping:** At least **10% of plot area** must be reserved for greening and soft landscaping [460].
* **Rainwater Harvesting:** Mandatory installation of rainwater harvesting storage tanks to supplement conventional municipal supply [459, 460].
* **Driveway Separation:** Adjacent driveways on neighboring plots must be separated by at least **1.5 metres of soft landscaping** [464].

---

### 2.3 Residential Neighborhood Access & Subdivision Rules [171–175, 271–274, 464]

* **Minimum Subplot Size:** Resultant subplots in any urban subdivision scheme must not be smaller than **0.05 hectares (500 square metres)** [172].
* **Road Frontage Rule:** Subplots of 0.05 hectares must **not front directly onto major roads measuring 18 metres or wider** [173].
* **Subdivision Plot Angle:** Subplots must meet access streets at right angles (90 degrees) [173].
* **Comprehensive Subdivision Threshold:** Any subdivision producing **more than 19 subplots** is classified as a *Comprehensive Subdivision Scheme*, requiring a topo-cadastral survey, planning brief, and full neighborhood planning principles [173, 174].
* **Road Hierarchy for Subdivisions [174]:**
  * Distributor roads: **15 metres road reserve** [174].
  * Secondary distributors: **12 metres road reserve** [174].
  * Local access roads leading to cul-de-sacs: **Minimum 9 metres road reserve** [174].
  * Road serving 1 to 8 plots: **9 metres road reserve** [274].
  * Road serving more than 8 plots: **12 metres road reserve** [274].
* **Cul-de-sac Limits:** Cul-de-sacs must serve no more than 10 residential plots, must not exceed **60 metres in length**, and must feature a turning circle of **at least 15 metres diameter** at the dead end [271, 272, 464].
* **Pedestrian Breaks:** Pedestrian footpaths of **3 to 6 metres width** must be provided after every 8 plots or every 100 metres in medium and high-density zones [174, 464].
* **Road Truncations:** Corner plot truncations must equal **half the width of the narrower adjoining road reserve** [172, 277].
* **Public Land Surrender:** Suitable land must be surrendered for public open spaces, utilities, and social amenities based on projected population density [176].

## 3. Commercial Core & Mixed-Use Zoning

Kilimani functions as an expanded commercial and business node complementary to Nairobi's Central Business District (CBD).

### 3.1 Commercial Land Allocation & Density Hierarchy [468, 471]

* **Urban Commercial Area Share:** Commercial land use should account for **5% to 10%** of total planned urban land area [468, 451].
* **Minimum Commercial Plot Size:** **0.045 hectares (450 square metres)** [468].
* **Maximum Plot Coverage:** **80%** (allowing 20% for covered verandas, arcades, and pedestrian walkways) [468].
* **Commercial Plot Ratio Hierarchy [471]:**
  * **Central Core:** Plot ratio of **600% (1:6)** for high-rise commercial towers centered around public open spaces or major transport nodes [471].
  * **Concentric Step-Down Zones:** Plot ratio decreases outwards from the core in steps of **150%**:
    * Core: **600% (1:6)** [471].
    * Tier 1 Buffer: **450% (1:4.5)** [471].
    * Tier 2 Buffer: **300% (1:3)** [471].
    * Transition Outer Zone: **150% (1:1.5)** [471].

---

### 3.2 Commercial Building Lines, Access & Infrastructure [468–471]

* **Minimum Commercial Street Width:** **15 metres** to accommodate high vehicular traffic, pedestrian sidewalks, and underground utilities [468, 469].
* **Major Link Roads:** Local distributor roads of **18 to 25 metres width** to connect commercial centers to arterial networks [469, 470].
* **Building Lines:** Minimum **6 metres setback** where fronting road reserves range between 15 metres and 18 metres width [469].
* **Rear Service Lanes:** Mandatory **6 metres wide service lane** at the rear of all commercial plots for loading, unloading, and utility access [470].
* **Direct Access Prohibition:** Direct vehicular access onto major arterial corridors is prohibited; access must be handled via loop streets, slip roads, and rear service lanes [470, 471].
* **Non-Motorized Transport (NMT):** Commercial corridors must incorporate dedicated **3 metres wide cycle lanes** and **2 metres wide pedestrian sidewalks** [470].
* **Underground Infrastructure:** All electrical, telecommunication, water, and sewer services in commercial zones must be laid underground [469, 470].
* **Greening Requirement:** Minimum **5% green cover** on all commercial plots [470].

---

### 3.3 Shopping Malls & Retail Complexes [478–482]

* **Minimum Land Requirement:** **2.0 hectares (20,000 square metres)** [478].
* **Access Road Minimum:** Direct frontage on an access road measuring **at least 15 metres width** with proximity to public transport [478].
* **Acceleration / Deceleration Lanes:** Mandatory **100 metres long acceleration and deceleration lanes** along primary road accesses [479, 495].
* **Pedestrian Safety & Crossings:** Clearly delineated crosswalks, raised speed humps, and vertical separation of pedestrian and vehicular traffic [479, 481].
* **Amenities & Facilities:** Public restrooms, dedicated lactation and baby changing rooms, emergency exits, fire assembly points, and drop-off zones [480, 481].
* **Greening & Open Spaces:** Minimum **10% greening / landscaping** on mall sites [481].

## 4. Light Industry, Petrol Stations & Service Facilities

In high-density urban areas like Kilimani, industrial uses are strictly limited to non-polluting light industries, service workshops, and petrol stations.

### 4.1 Petrol Service Stations (Tiers 1 to 4) [492–496]

Petrol stations are classified under light industrial user due to environmental and fire safety risks [492].

| **Car Wash / Lubrication Bay** | Prohibited | Permitted | Permitted | Permitted |

#### 4.1.2 Siting & Safety Setbacks for Petrol Stations [494–496]:
* **Distance Between Stations:** Minimum **500 metres separation** between petrol stations on the same side of a road [494].
* **Distance to Social Facilities:** Minimum **100 metres clearance** from schools, churches, libraries, hospitals, playgrounds, and public halls [495].
* **Pump Setback from Road Edge:** Minimum **15 metres setback** from the edge of the road reserve to the nearest pump [495].
* **Setback from Residential Buildings:** Minimum **30 metres buffer** between petrol pumps and any residential structure [495].
* **Perimeter Wall:** Mandatory **3.0 metres high masonry perimeter wall** along rear and side plot boundaries [495].
* **Acceleration / Deceleration Lanes:** **80 to 100 metres long** acceleration and deceleration lanes required along major roads [495, 496].
* **Environmental Permits:** Mandatory NEMA Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) and periodic Environmental Audits (EA) [494].

### 4.2 Jua Kali & Service Workshops [490, 491]

* **Minimum Plot Size:** **0.25 hectares (2,500 square metres)** [490].
* **Distance from Petrol Stations:** Workshops handling flammable materials must be located **at least 100 metres away from petrol stations** [491].
* **Infrastructure Requirements:** Separate entrance and exit gates, hard-standing surfaces, solid waste collection bays, common sanitation blocks, fire hydrants, and fire extinguishers [490, 491].

## 5. Comprehensive Infrastructure Guidelines

Kilimani's rapid high-rise urban development requires robust physical and utility infrastructure.

### 5.1 Road Network & Access Hierarchy [263–274]

Urban roads are classified according to mobility versus access functions:

1. **Urban Major Arterials (Class H):** Primary mobility corridors carrying through-traffic across the city.
   * **Road Reserve Width:** **60 to 90 metres** [266, 267].
   * Direct plot access is strictly prohibited [275].
2. **Urban Minor Arterials (Class J):** Link different urban zones and principal bus routes.
   * **Road Reserve Width:** **40 to 60 metres** [267].
3. **Urban Major Collector Roads (Class K):** Collect traffic from local access streets and feed arterials.
   * **Road Reserve Width:** **25 to 40 metres** [268].
4. **Urban Minor Collector Roads (Class L):** Collect local neighborhood traffic.
   * **Road Reserve Width:** **15 to 30 metres** [269, 270].
5. **Local Urban Access Roads (Class M & N):** Direct property access for commercial and residential plots.
   * **Road Reserve Width:** **12 to 15 metres** [270].
6. **Residential / Commercial Access Streets (Class P):** Direct access to clusters of residential plots.
   * **Road Reserve Width:** **9 to 15 metres** [271].

---

### 5.2 Mass Rapid Transit & Transport Interchanges [278–284]

* **Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) Corridors:** Minimum **65 metres road reserve** for roads incorporating dedicated central BRT lanes, median stations, and pedestrian footbridges [278].
* **Light Rail Transit (LRT):**
  * Station Spacing: **1.5 to 3.0 kilometres** [279].
  * Maintenance Depot Site: Minimum **5.0 hectares** [279].
* **Intermodal Interchanges:** Located at major urban nodes to integrate bus, LRT, taxi, NMT, and private vehicles. Residential uses must be set back from interchange hubs [281, 282].
* **Bus Termini:** Located outside the Central Business District core to prevent gridlock, equipped with passenger shelters, public toilets, security booths, and waste facilities [284].

---

### 5.3 Off-Street & On-Plot Parking Requirements [285–291]

#### 5.3.1 Parking Ratios by Land Use [286–289]

| Land Use Category | Minimum Prescribed Parking Ratio |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Residential — Studio / Bedsitter** | 1 car parking space per 10 housing units [287] |
| **Residential — 1 Bedroom Unit** | 1 car parking space per 3 housing units [287] |
| **Residential — 2+ Bedroom Units** | 1 car parking space per 2 housing units [287] |
| **Residential — Visitor Parking** | Additional 25% of total required parking reserved for visitors [287] |
| **Office & Administrative Space** | 1 car parking space per 50 square metres GFA (Outside CBD) or per 200 sq m GFA (In CBD) [288, 432] |
| **Shopping Malls & Commercial Retail** | 10% of total site area dedicated to parking [288] |
| **Hotels & Hospitality** | 1 car parking space per guest room [288] |
| **Hospitals & Medical Centers** | 1 car parking space per 2 hospital beds [288] |
| **Places of Worship (Churches / Mosques)** | 1 car parking space per 3 persons at maximum seating capacity [288] |
| **Restaurants & Recreational Facilities** | 1 car parking space per 3 persons at maximum capacity [288] |
| **Industrial / Storage Space** | 1 car parking space (21 sq m) per 100 square metres industrial space [289] |

#### 5.3.2 Standard Vehicle Bay Dimensions [286]:
* **Car Bay (Parallel / Angle Parking):** **5.0 to 6.5 metres length by 2.5 metres width** (Standard area = 21 square metres) [286, 288].
* **Bus Bay:** **10.0 metres length by 3.3 metres width** [286].
* **Truck / Trailer Bay (30-degree angle):** **40.0 metres length by 2.5 metres width** [286].

#### 5.3.3 Persons Living with Disabilities (PLWD) Parking [289, 290]:
* **Bay Dimensions:** Minimum width of **3.0 metres** located adjacent to ramps, elevators, and main building entrances [289].
* **Quota:** At least **1 space or 1% of total parking spaces** (whichever is greater) [290].

#### 5.3.4 Bicycle Parking Standards [290, 291]:
* **Bay Dimensions:** **2.0 metres length by 1.0 metre width** per bicycle [290].
* **Land Use Allocations:** Residential (20% of total parking area), Commercial (10%), Educational (20%), Recreational (50%), Public Purpose (30%) [290, 291].

### 5.4 Non-Motorized Transport (NMT) Infrastructure [291–293]

* **Combined Cycle Lane & Walkway:** Minimum **3.0 metres width** [292].
* **Dedicated Cycle Lane:** Minimum **2.0 metres width** [292].
* **Dedicated Pedestrian Sidewalk:** Minimum **2.0 metres width** [292].
* **Urban Major Arterials (Class H):** Minimum **5.0 metres NMT corridor** (incorporating 2m sidewalk, 2m cycle lane, street furniture, lighting, and tree lining) [292, 293].

### 5.5 Public Utilities Wayleaves & Reticulation [337–379]

#### 5.5.1 Water Supply & Reticulation Network [337–353]
* **Average Domestic Water Requirement:** **20 litres per person per day** [339].
* **Pipe Burial Depths:** Large distribution mains buried at least **1.2 metres deep**; secondary service pipes at least **1.0 metre deep** [341].
* **Wayleave Width:** **3.0 metres wide wayleave** along road reserves [378].
* **Water Points Walking Distance:** Maximum walking distance of **200 metres** from any residential doorstep to a public water point [349].
* **Borehole Siting Rules:** Minimum plot size **0.05 hectares (500 sq m)**, minimum **10 metres protection buffer**, **50 metres setback** from septic tanks/soak pits, and minimum **800 metres separation distance** between adjacent boreholes [351, 352].

#### 5.5.2 Sewerage & Wastewater Management [358–363]
* **Sewer Connection Mandate:** Required for all settlements with populations exceeding 3,000 [359].
* **Septic Tank Limit:** Septic tanks are **strictly prohibited for buildings exceeding 4 storeys / floors**; high-rise developments must connect to public trunk sewers or install approved decentralized wastewater treatment plants / biodigesters [359].
* **Sewer Treatment Plant Buffers:** **3 to 5 kilometres separation** from built-up urban settlements and **300 metres buffer** from water bodies [359].

#### 5.5.3 Stormwater Drainage & Detention [364–367]
* **Land Area Share:** **10% of total land area** must be allocated to stormwater management infrastructure [365].
* **Drainage Wayleave:** Minimum **3.0 metres wayleave** for retention/detention basins and drain alignments [365].
* **CBD & Commercial Coverage:** All stormwater drains in commercial centers must be **fully covered** and designed for peak storm discharge [366].

#### 5.5.4 Electricity Wayleaves & Clearances [255–263, 378, 379]
* **Overhead Line Wayleaves (Trace Widths) [379]:**
  * Low / Medium Voltage (11kV to 33kV): **10 metres trace width** (5m on either side of centerline) [379].
  * High Voltage (66kV): **20 metres trace width** [379].
  * Transmission Lines (132kV, 220kV, 400kV, 500kV HVDC): **60 metres trace width** [379].
* **Horizontal Clearance to Fuel / Gas Tanks:** Minimum **15 metres clearance** for voltages up to 220kV; **20 metres clearance** for 400kV lines [255, 256].
* **Underground Power Cables:** Minimum **0.6 metres surface-to-surface separation** from other services in joint ducts [260].
* **Substation Plot Requirements:**
  * Main Receiving Substation (275kV): **1.6 hectares** [262].
  * Distribution Substation: **0.20 hectares** [262].
  * Local Single / Double Chamber Substation: **0.05 hectares (500 sq m)** [262].
  * Substation Buffer: **50 metres buffer** from residential boundaries [263].

#### 5.5.5 Telecommunications & Fiber Optics [320–332, 378]
* **Wayleave Width:** **2.0 metres wayleave** along road reserves for fiber optic cables and telephone lines [378].
* **Underground Cable Depth:** Laid on the opposite side of the road reserve from power cables with a clearance of **0.46 to 1.22 metres** [324].
* **Telecom Masts & Base Station Towers [326–332]:**
  * Minimum Land Area: **0.05 hectares (500 square metres)** [326].
  * Tower Setback: Equal to **1.5 times the tower height** from any residential boundary or property line [326].
  * Tower Height Limit in Residential Zones: **Maximum 25 metres height limit** for masts in residential areas [330].
  * Overall Maximum Tower Height: **150 metres** (subject to KCAA clearance) [329].
  * Inter-Tower Separation: Minimum **2.0 kilometres distance** between new towers and existing masts [329].
  * Power Line Safety Distance: Located at a distance of at least **120% of tower height** from high-voltage power lines [331].
  * Prohibited Siting: School grounds, health facilities, and flood-prone zones [332].

## 6. Social Infrastructure Standards

High-density urban redevelopment requires structured provision of public social amenities proportional to population size.

### 6.1 Educational Facilities [383–404]

#### 6.1.1 Pre-Primary Schools (ECDE) [388–390]:
* **Catchment Population:** 1 pre-primary school per **2,500 population** [390].
* **Maximum Walking Distance:** **500 metres** [390].
* **Minimum Plot Area:** **0.20 to 0.25 hectares** (Can be reduced to **0.15 hectares** in urban high-density / informal areas provided safety and play standards are met) [389, 390].
* **Building Height Restriction:** Pre-primary classrooms must be on the **Ground Floor only** (Ground + 1 allowed for admin offices) [389, 390].

#### 6.1.2 Primary Schools (Comprehensive Grades 1 to 9) [391–395]:
* **Catchment Population:** **5,000 population** (urban) [391].
* **Maximum Walking Distance:** **2.0 kilometres** [392].
* **Inter-School Separation:** Maximum **4.0 kilometres** between adjacent schools [392].
* **Minimum Plot Sizes [391, 392]:**
  * Single Stream (Day): **2.0 hectares** [391].
  * Double Stream (Day): **3.9 hectares** [391].
  * Private / Urban Constrained High-Rise Primary School: Minimum **0.20 hectares** (Subject to strict safety, light, and play facility compliance) [392].
* **Maximum Building Storeys:** Maximum **Ground + 3 Floors** with mandatory dual staircases and elevators / ramps [392].

#### 6.1.3 Secondary Schools (Senior Secondary) [395–397]:
* **Catchment Population:** **8,000 population** (urban) [395].
* **Maximum Walking Distance:** **3.0 kilometres** [395].
* **Minimum Plot Sizes:** 1 Stream (**2.0 ha**), 2 Streams (**2.5 ha**), 3 Streams (**3.5 ha**) [395].
* **Maximum Building Storeys:** Maximum **Ground + 4 Floors** [396].

### 6.3 Child Daycare Facilities [383–388]
* **Catchment Population:** 1 facility per **5,000 population** [386].

## 7. Urban Greening & Environmental Protection

High-density urban cores must integrate green infrastructure to mitigate urban heat islands and preserve ecological integrity.

### 7.1 Urban Greening & Skyrise Strategies [523–527]

* **Minimum Plot Green Coverage:** Mandatory **10% soft landscaping / green cover** on all residential, commercial, and industrial plots [460, 484, 525].
* **Skyrise & Roof Greening:** Installation of rooftop gardens and horizontal greening on structural slabs encouraged for high-density multi-storey towers [523, 524].
* **Vertical Greening:** Green walls and climbing plants on building facades to improve visual aesthetics and microclimate [524].
* **Utility Clearance from Trees:** Trees with penetrating roots must not be planted within **3.0 metres** of water mains or sewer lines (1.5m for pipes under 600mm diameter), and at least **1.5 metres clearance** from manholes and hydrants [526, 527].
